Know the boundaries

Browser-local
Data stays on this browser and device. There is no account or cloud sync.

Not encrypted
Local storage and JSON backups are not a secure vault. Avoid shared devices and unredacted secrets.

No automatic contact
DropProof does not contact a seller, carrier, insurer, or payment provider.

Not a determination
This is a record-keeping aid, not legal advice or proof that a claim will succeed.
